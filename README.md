# Pride & Prejudice (2005)

## Overview
**Pride & Prejudice** is a romantic period drama adapted from *Jane Austen's* classic 1813 novel. Directed by Joe Wright, the film follows the turbulent relationship between Elizabeth Bennet and the proud Mr. Darcy in 19th-century England.

---

## Why I Love It
* **Cinematography & Music:** The sweeping countryside shots and *Dario Marianelli's* piano score create an unforgettable atmosphere.
* **Chemistry:** The slow-burn tension between Elizabeth and Mr. Darcy makes every scene captivating.
* **Timeless Story:** It is a witty and emotional tale about overcoming first impressions and social pride.

## Top 3 Favorite Characters
1. **Elizabeth Bennet** – Witty, independent, and unafraid to speak her mind.
2. **Mr. Fitzwilliam Darcy** – Reserved and misunderstood at first, yet deeply loyal.
3. **Mr. Bennet** – Dryly humorous and fiercely supportive of Elizabeth.
